# WaveHat rev B test pads

Generated by `gen_docs.py` from `wavehat_design.py`. All on the **bottom** side, 1.0 mm round (TP1, TP2 1.5 mm), numbered on the silkscreen as TPn. Positions are mm from the board's lower-left corner **seen from the component side** (the Gerber and pick-and-place origin); the bottom-view x, as you look at the pads, is 21 minus that.

Each pad is on a net that crosses between layers, so a reading from the pad to its component-side partner proves that net's vias (twinned in rev B). TP22, TP23 and TP8 also let the power stage run from a bench supply before a XIAO is fitted: feed 12 V between J4 pad 2 and pad 3, expect about 5.1 V on TP22, 4.7 V on TP23, and 3V3 on TP8 only once a XIAO is on.
