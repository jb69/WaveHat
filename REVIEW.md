# WaveHat: does the plan and algorithm meet the objective? (review, 21 Oct 2026)

How this was made: six readers mapped the objective, the algorithm, the firmware, the delivery chain, the hardware and the data on hand; six critics (wave physics, DSP, validation, integration, hardware, devil's advocate) judged the plan against the objective; every finding was then attacked by two independent verifiers (one checking the cited files, one the reasoning); a final pass wrote this up and a completeness check (§8) looked for what it missed. 34 merged findings: 19 confirmed, 13 contested, 2 refuted. The lever-arm figures in G1 were then reproduced with the project's own simulator (`firmware/desktop/lever_arm.py`, results in `firmware/desktop/results/lever_arm.md`); the other critic-computed numbers (G3, G6, G10) come from the critics' own re-runs and are not stored, so treat them as estimates until `seasim.py` gains the corresponding terms.

Path shorthand: `W` = `projects/wavehat`, `R` = `reference` (both under `raspberry-pi-boat-computer/`). Line numbers are as of 21 Oct 2026.

## 1. The objective, as checkable requirements

- **REQ-1 Measure**: turn a yacht's motion into Hs, Tp, Tz and heave (`W/README.md:3`).
- **REQ-2 Deliver to Signal K**: environment.heave at ~4 Hz (standard); environment.waves.significantHeight / period (Tp) / meanPeriod (Tz) / underway every ~79 s (proposed paths) (`W/firmware/README.md:11-17`).
- **REQ-3 Time to result**: first Hs/Tp ~10 min after start, full 20-min window at ~21 min (`W/firmware/README.md:19`).
- **REQ-4 Accuracy**: "about ±20–30 % on Hs at anchor" until calibrated against a buoy or forecast; no figure for Tp, Tz or heave at sea (`W/README.md:44`, `W/firmware/README.md:58`).
- **REQ-5 Conditions**: periods only valid at anchor/drifting; underway readings flagged; direction "later, approximate only" (`W/README.md:38,45-46`).
- **REQ-6 Log on board**: one CSV line per result in /waves.csv on the 256 MB SD-NAND (`W/firmware/src/wave_log.h:31-32`).
- **REQ-7 NMEA 2000 node**: listen-only, six single-frame PGNs incl. SOG (`W/firmware/README.md:97`).
- **REQ-8 Radar feed**: ~/.swell.json {t, hs_ft, period_s}, refreshed ≤10 min, drawn only with anchor watch armed (`R/sailing-exodus/01-radar/source/README.md:99`; `radar-display.py:2174,2312`).
- **REQ-9/10 Hardware**: XIAO C3/C6/S3 carrier, rigid mount, 5–30 V, CAN, storage; same board runs VESC Express as a Takuma v2 candidate (`W/hardware/DESIGN.md:21-34`).

## 3. Verdict

| Aspect | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hs accuracy at anchor (±20–30 %) | **unknown** | Maths+sensor proven to 3 % on synthetic seas only; the two dominant real errors (IMU lever arm, hull RAO) are unmodelled and uncompensated, and either alone can exceed the budget (F1, F3). No real data, no acceptance test. |
| Tp | **partly** | −1 % bias, 3.8 % RMS single-peaked; pins to 22.5 s for Tp ≥ 20 s and in flat calm; wind sea wins over swell in bimodal seas; ±10 % scatter per 20-min window. |
| Tz | **partly** | ±4 % vs band truth; +5 to +20 % vs full spectrum at Tp 3–5 s, which the docs state only qualitatively; buoys are band-limited too, so field impact is small. |
| Heave (environment.heave) | **partly** | Steady-state amplitude within 0.25 %, phase lead 24–42° at 8–14 s; metres of false heave for 2–3 min after every boot/ODR restart; never produced by a real sensor. |
| Underway flag | **fails (as implemented)** | 1.5 kn SOG threshold is physically right, but OR of per-hop maxima latches on one 250 ms spike for 18.4–19.7 min and blanks the radar; absent flag read as "at anchor". |
| Signal K delivery | **partly** | Values will flow; metadata for all five paths is dropped on every connect (first delta ~1.87 kB > 1024 B buffer); never connected with an IMU present. |
| Radar swell display | **partly** | Format and units correct; stale data re-stamped fresh for up to 25 min; "SWELL 0.0 ft 22 s" in calm; radar reads /home/pi hard-coded; chain never run end to end. |
| Logging | **partly** | Results-only CSV, untested on chip, no raw/decimated IMU, UTC only with internet. |
| NMEA 2000 | **partly** | Decoder correct in loopback; never on a transceiver or real bus; fast-packet not reassembled. |
| eFoil / VESC Express | **partly** | Builds for C3/C6/S3, boots on bare C3; CAN and storage untested. |
| Validation evidence | **fails** | Self-referential simulation; the only field step has no reference, duration or pass/fail; no slot to apply the result. |
| Direction | **meets** | Scoped out; bridge writes no from_true_deg; radar degrades to a label. |

## 4. Confirmed gaps

**G1. IMU lever arm unspecified, unmodelled, uncompensated** (major). The IMU measures z_imu = z_cg + y·roll + x·pitch; the simulator places it on the roll/pitch axis (`seasim.py:183-185`), the firmware has no offset term (`wavemath.h:23-37`), and placement guidance is only "rigid, square, bow forward, low" (`W/enclosure/README.md:20-23`, `DESIGN.md:28,186`). Reproduced with the project's own simulator plus the lever-arm term (`firmware/desktop/lever_arm.py`, `results/lever_arm.md`): with the IMU 1 m off the centreline, Hs reads +27 % high in a 1 m / 6 s sea with 5° RMS roll, +87 % with 15° roll, and +10 % in a 2 m / 8 s sea; 0.3 m off gives +8 %, +24 % and +3 %; 5 m forward of the pitch centre gives −8 to −13 %. Tp is barely affected (within 4 %). Effect: a nav-station or quarter-berth install alone consumes the ±20–30 % budget, pulls Tp toward the roll period, and no scalar hull factor can absorb it. Fix: mounting rule (within 0.3 m of centreline, low, within ~1 m of midships, bolted to structure); record r=(x,y,z) at installation and in /waves.csv; add r to seasim.py; optionally compensate a_cg = f − α×r − ω×(ω×r) using the gyro already read.

**G2. No real-sensor evidence, no acceptance protocol, no place for the hull/site factor** (major). All error figures come from evaluate.py on seasim.py with an inert sensor model (ideal vs calibrated Hs differ ≤1.1 %); the only field step is "compare with a buoy or forecast for a few days, note the hull's factor" (`W/firmware/README.md:192`); publish() emits Hs/Tp/Tz unconditionally and the only ConfigItem is accelerometer calibration (`W/firmware/src/main.cpp:152-155,298-305`). Single 20-min comparisons scatter ±5–8 % (1σ) from sampling alone. Effect: REQ-4 cannot be passed or failed; a correction needs a firmware rebuild. Fix: §6 validation plan; a `Sea-state calibration` ConfigItem with hs_scale (default 1.0) applied before publish, raw and scaled both logged.

**G3. Hull heave RAO unmodelled; scalar factor cannot represent it** (major). seasim.py sets heave = surface (`:5-6,140`) and rescales roll to fixed RMS (`:151-158`). A 10–15 m hull in head seas follows ~0.2–0.5 of 3 s chop, ~0.9 at 5 s, ≈1 above 6 s: measured/true Hs −40 to −70 % at Tp 2.5–3.5 s, −8 to −22 % at 6 s, <−10 % at 8 s. Effect: the ±20–30 % figure holds for swell (T ≥ 6 s) but not for harbour chop; a factor fitted on a chop day is ~2× wrong on a swell day. Fix: scope significantHeight to the swell band or publish a swell/wind-sea partition; make any factor period-banded; log the 72-bin spectrum per hop (~0.3 MB/day) so it can be fitted.

**G4. Underway latch** (major). underway = OR over 14 per-hop SOG maxima sampled every 250 ms (`main.cpp:44,143-164,224-225`). One sample >0.77 m/s (sheering in a gust, GPS jitter) latches 18.4–19.7 min; bridge stops writing within 60 s, radar blank from about t0+10–12 min to t0+18–21 min (next hop ≤79 s, bridge ≤60 s, radar holds the file 600 s); recurring surges keep it permanently blank. A 10 s surge is 0.85 % of the 1181 s window and cannot distort periods. Fix: per hop store mean SOG and fraction >0.77 m/s; underway = window mean >0.77 or fraction >25 %; publish window-mean SOG; do not add STW (an anchored boat in a stream measures the same absolute period a moored buoy does).

**G5. Nothing downstream of the IMU has run end to end** (major). poll_imu() returns before any set() when imu_ok is false (`main.cpp:179-189,226`), so the bare-C3 runs published nothing; bridge tests use a hand-built dict (`test_swell_bridge.py:18-45`); the checklist's only Signal K item is "rocking the box moves environment.heave" (`W/firmware/README.md:191`): nothing verifies the environment.waves.* values or their metadata on the server, and nothing covers the bridge or the radar. Real seam already visible: radar reads `/home/pi/.swell.json` (`radar-display.py:768`), bridge writes `~/.swell.json` (`swell_bridge.py:85`). Fix: a `-D WAVEHAT_SIM_IMU` env feeding a 0.50 m / 8.0 s displacement sinusoid (0.31 m/s² amplitude) at 26 Hz; pass = significantHeight 1.41 m ±3 %, period 8.0 ±0.3 s with units in meta, bridge writes hs_ft 4.6 / period_s 8.0, radar shows "SWELL 4.6 ft 8 s" and clears 10 min after the bridge stops; SOG 2 kn for 5 min then 0 kn → flag true within 79 s, clears after 14 hops.

**G6. Vibration aliasing with LPF2 off** (major by evidence, minor by domain). CTRL1_XL = 0x28, LPF2_XL_EN = 0 (`W/firmware/lib/lsm6dso/src/lsm6dso.cpp:70`); the 1.2 Hz anti-alias is software after sampling. Anything within ±0.5 Hz of 26/52/78 Hz surviving the undocumented LPF1 folds into the wave band: through the real pipeline 1 mg at 0.10 Hz reads Hs 0.071 m, at 0.05 Hz 0.29 m; 10 mg at 0.05 Hz 2.9 m. Sources: 4-cyl diesel at 765–795 or 1530–1590 rpm, 50 Hz genset running 2–6 % fast. LPF1 is probably a decimator with deep notches at k·ODR, so the real effect may be millimetres, but nothing on hand says so. Fix: ODR 104 Hz + firmware low-pass + decimate ×4 (CPU 0.4 → ~1.5 %); bench sweep 20–30 Hz at bring-up, pass Hs < 2 cm; log engine state beside results.

**G7. Band edge and flat-calm periods** (major/minor). Implemented band is 0.0444–0.4951 Hz, not the documented 0.04–0.5 Hz (`wavemath.py:209-213`, `main.cpp:277`, `wavemath.h:31`). Tp pins to 22.5055 s whenever the smoothed peak lands on bin 0: +12.5 % at true 20 s, −10 % at 25 s, and in every flat-calm run with Hs 0.5 cm (`results.csv:362-373`); Tz reads 10–15 s. Bridge forwards it; radar prints "SWELL 0.0 ft 22 s" in every marina. Fix: publish Tp/Tz as null when Hs < 0.05 m or the peak is on an edge bin or <3× the band median; fix the three documentation strings; keep f_lo (bin 6 only moves the pin to 26 s).

**G8. Signal K metadata dropped on every connect** (major/minor). heave_out->set() runs from boot (`main.cpp:226,318`), so at first connect the 20-entry queue is full and the metadata-bearing first delta is 1712–1932 B against the 1024 B default (`signalk_ws_client.h:45-46`), dropped without re-arming (`signalk_ws_client.cpp:1546-1566`). Not seen on the bare C3 because no IMU meant no heave. Fix: `-D SENSESP_SK_WS_BUFFER_SIZE=4096`; checklist item: Data Browser shows units on environment.waves.*.

**G9. Stale swell re-stamped fresh** (major/minor). Bridge accepts server data 900 s old and writes t = now (`swell_bridge.py:36,64-69`); radar holds 600 s more: a dead WaveHat is drawn as live for 25 min, describing a window centred ~35 min back. Absent/stale underway is read as at-anchor (`:61,67`); untested. Fix: t = min(hs.ts, tp.ts); MAX_AGE_S 480–600; skip when underway is absent unless `--assume-anchored`; tests for absent/stale-true/stale-false.

**G10. Heave boot transient** (major/minor). Heave biquads unprimed (`wavemath.cpp:246-252`), Mahony bias from zero, new_processor() discards state on ODR restart (`main.cpp:127-133,236-239`). Simulated: 0.5–4.4 m false heave peaks for 2–3 min after boot, 0.6–3.7 m after a restart on a 1 m sea. Hs/Tp unaffected. Fix: keep Mahony/biquad state across restarts, estimate gyro bias from the first 2 s, prime heave filters, hold heave NaN for 180 s; assert on stats[0] and early heave in tests.

**G11. C6 never executed** (major/minor). All evidence is C3; self-test env is C3-only (`platformio.ini:56-60`). C6 pins are the safer set (no strapping pins on the hat) and it has 512 KB SRAM, so failure is unlikely, but a fault after soldering to an OSP board costs diagnosis time. Fix: add `[env:xiao_c6_n2k_selftest]`, run xiao_c6 on a bare C6 against the real server for 24 h before soldering.

**Smaller confirmed items**: roll/pitch computed but never published; navigation.attitude and a roll spectral peak would flag the "Tp near roll period" case README.md:44 already names (F15). environment.waves.* conflicts with the environment.water.waves.* OpenCPN already consumes (`R/opencpn/source/model/src/ais_decoder.cpp:2466-2486`); rename now, ~15 edits, nothing depends on it (F19). Six-face calibration on the finished box is unreliable: the mounting ears tilt the side faces about 27° and the PG7 gland on the bow wall tilts the end faces, so X and Y scales come out wrong (a 1.12 scale error → Hs +20 to +80 % at 15° roll); the README's primary instruction is to calibrate before closing the box, which is right, and there is no range check on the entered values in `accel_calibration.h:42-49` (F21). Checklist has no quantitative motion test and nothing exercises the gyro (F23). Harness checks only stats[-1] (F26). Power stage never energised before a XIAO is committed; probe U4 pads 12/13/14 (F27). Bridge prints "Signal K not reachable: HTTP 404" for the first 9 min after every boot (F29).

### Contested findings

- **F7 bimodal Tp**: evidence lens confirms the wind sea wins deterministically (78/78 updates) so the radar labels 4.1 ft/5 s as "SWELL" under 0.4 m/14 s swell; domain lens says Hs=4√m0 and dominant-period are the standard buoy definitions and the objective never promised a partition. Both agree a swell/wind-sea split is a cheap enhancement.
- **F9 ODR bias**: +0.21 % systematic bias confirmed (`main.cpp:232-233`), but it costs −0.4 % Hs; the near-universal 2-min restart is the 0.5 % threshold against a few-percent oscillator, not the bias. One-line fix.
- **F10 no raw IMU log**: evidence lens: nothing from the first trial is re-processable; domain lens: /waves.csv plus the 4 Hz heave stream on Signal K suffice for the scalar factor the plan states. Raw logging matters only if G1/G3/G6 changes are expected, which this review says they are.
- **F18 scope creep**: the two ticked checklist items are the only ones runnable without boards; N2K, logging and VESC are in the objective. What survives: freeze one bring-up configuration.
- **F20, F24, F25, F28, F30, F33, F34**: documentation hygiene (stale "not ordered", ENIG vs OSP, Tz band not in metadata, 24° heave lead undocumented, OSP within rated reflow count, N2K-bus power is consistent with an anchor watch that needs the bus live, heading already logged every 79 s). Fix in one pass, none affects the objective.
- **F22 sensor model / self-test**: 0.5 mg wander at 0.05 Hz would read 0.14 m but is 5–10× pessimistic for the LSM6DSO class; keep as a 60-min still-bench check (Hs < 0.03 m).
- **F16 C3 GPIO 9 (BOOT button = SD MISO)**: both verifiers refuted the reboot/factory-reset mechanism: SensESP's button poll and the SD transactions run in the same loop thread and every SD call is blocking, so the poll never samples the pin mid-transaction, and between transactions the 10 kΩ pull-up holds it high. What remains (seen on the bench, 21 Oct) is the Arduino "IO 9 is not set as GPIO" warning flood after `SPI.begin` re-muxes the pin, and a button whose reads are undefined. Set the C3 build's button to −1 (or another pin) and keep factory reset on the web UI. C6 and S3 are unaffected.
- **F32 alternatives**: a BNO085 on the Pi shares the hull response and cannot resolve the ±20–30 % term; the boards are ordered. Worth exposing wavemath.py as a plugin later.

## 5. Changes to the algorithm

1. **Mounting offset**: add `Config.r_imu[3]` (m, body frame); compute α from a differenced, 1.2 Hz low-passed gyro; a_cg = f − α×r − ω×(ω×r) before the Mahony rotation. Add the same term to seasim.py and re-run with r = (0.5, 2, 0) and (1, 0, 0) m. Test first in the simulator: lever-arm case error must drop from +30–60 % to <5 %.
2. **Quality gate**: Tp, Tz null when Hs < 0.05 m, or peak on bin 0/n−1, or smoothed peak < 3× band median. Add `environment.waves.quality` (segments/14, calibration present, underway known, peak ratio).
3. **Swell/wind-sea partition** at 0.1–0.125 Hz (or 1.5× wind-sea peak): publish swell.* and windSea.* Hs/Tp; the bridge sends the swell partition.
4. **Anti-alias**: ODR 104 Hz, 4th-order low-pass, decimate ×4 to 26 Hz before the existing chain; redesign biquads for the new fs.
5. **Start-up**: prime heave biquads; gyro bias from first 2 s; Kp 0.5/Ki 0.01 for 30 s then 0.05/0.0006; keep state across ODR restarts; measure ODR for 120 s before constructing the processor and count from the first empty-FIFO poll.
6. **Underway statistic**: per-hop mean SOG and fraction above 0.77 m/s; keep 1.5 kn; flag periods only.
7. **Outputs**: navigation.attitude at 4 Hz; per hop roll RMS, roll peak, roll spectral peak period; warn when Tp is within ±20 % of the roll period. hs_scale ConfigItem applied before publish, raw and scaled logged.
8. **Documentation of the implemented band** 0.044–0.495 Hz in README, metadata and the spec proposal; period = Tp, meanPeriod = Tz stated explicitly.

## 7. What was checked and found sound

- Welch design: N 512, hop 256, periodic Hann, 2/(fs Σw²) scaling, 14 segments ≈ 27 DOF/bin; inherent Hs std ~3.5 %, matching the 3.1 % RMS. Rectangle-rule moments in double; Hs = 4√m0, Tz = √(m0/m2) correct.
- Heave filter HP4/s² at 0.02 Hz: |H/ideal| 0.9976–0.9999 across 2–25 s; double precision needed and used.
- 1.2 Hz anti-alias and decimate ×8: gain 0.9996 at 0.5 Hz; only 2.76–3.75 Hz content folds into the band, at −29 to −42 dB.
- Mahony Kp 0.05 / Ki 0.0006: 20 s tilt constant, bias loop ζ 1.02; wave acceleration biases gravity only at second order (<0.002 m/s² in band). Yaw/mounting inversion irrelevant to Hs/Tp/Tz.
- Six-face offset/scale calibration is the right model; cross-axis and tempco are second order (~2 mg over 20 °C).
- White-noise floor 4.8–5.1 mm analytic vs 4.6–5.7 mm simulated.
- 1.5 kn SOG threshold: encounter shift ±4–16 % at 3–14 s, within Tp noise; Hs invariant to it. SOG, not STW, is right for buoy comparison.
- Units chain: m → 0.1 ft, Tp → 0.1 s → integer s; no mismatch.
- Freshness while running: ≤139 s against 600 s (4.3×). Server-restart recovery ≤79 s.
- Power chain: PTC → SS14 → SMBJ30A → LMR51606 (5.16 V with 22 kΩ) → SS14, holds from 5.6 V in. ## 8. Additions from the completeness check

A final agent compared the review above with the subsystem maps and the files, and found these gaps in the review itself. They are included here unverified, with their evidence, in rough order of weight.

1. **Set the clock from the bus.** The sea-trial plan needs UTC to match 20-minute windows to buoy records, but `/waves.csv` gets UTC only once NTP has run (`wave_log.h`), and an anchorage may have no internet. The NMEA 2000 listener already reads the bus: decode PGN 126992 (System Time, single frame) and set the clock from it. Also: `wave_log.h` turns itself off silently after one failed open, and `SD.begin(..., format_if_empty)` would reformat a FAT corrupted by a power cut, erasing the history. Log the failure; don't auto-format after the first boot.
2. **Which period the radar gets.** Neither the Exodus README nor the Weather API says whether `period_s` / `wavePeriod` is peak or mean. The bridge sends Tp, the noisiest output (±10 %, −18 % worst), and the radar rounds it to whole seconds, so 8 s swell flickers 7–9 s between updates; Tz is within 0.2–4 % of truth. Decide (Tz is the stabler choice for a display; Tp is what forecasts quote) and say so in the bridge, the README and the spec proposal.
3. **Flash and heap budget for §5.** Flash is at 89 % (C3) / 92 % (C6) of the app slot and free heap 86 KB; `benchmark()` allocates about 56 KB transiently 5 s after boot. The §5 additions (partition, quality path, attitude outputs, 104 Hz ODR, spectrum logging, raw IMU logging, hs_scale) have no size estimate, and the C6 with its Wi-Fi 6 stack has never run: check `pio run -e xiao_c6` sizes as each lands, and consider dropping the 4096-point FFT from the benchmark.
4. **Signal K access for the bridge.** The server's default `allow_readonly` is false, so an unauthenticated bridge gets HTTP 401, which the bridge reports as "not reachable"; the README says to set `SIGNALK_TOKEN` but not which kind (user tokens expire; a device token does not); the suggested user systemd unit needs `loginctl enable-linger` to run at boot. Zero-hardware items for this week's list.
5. **Delta-queue eviction during Wi-Fi outages.** SensESP keeps 20 deltas and heave is set every 250 ms, so within 5 s of a disconnect the queued Hs/Tp/Tz/underway are evicted and the next values arrive at the next hop (≤79 s); a reconnect also re-triggers the metadata drop in G8. Send heave only while connected, or send it less often.
6. **Thermal keep-out not built.** `DESIGN.md` §5 requires a copper keep-out line between the sensor zone and the rest of the board; `gen_pcb.py` generates only the small rule area under the LGA and one continuous ground pour per layer, and the nearest heat source is U3 at 9.85 mm, not the buck. Add a warm-up drift measurement to bring-up (the 60-minute still bench starts after 21 min, by which time most heating has happened): start the still bench from cold.
7. **Pre-order items in `DESIGN.md` §13 with no record of being done**: widen the 3V3 feed to the IMU and transceiver (the IMU shares the XIAO's LDO with Wi-Fi TX bursts; the default track is 0.15 mm), review the auto-routed board, test-print the enclosure. Check the ordered Gerbers for the 3V3 width; if it is 0.15 mm, a noise-floor failure on the bench has a candidate cause.
8. **IMU assembly acceptance.** The 0.1 mm mask dam and X-ray of U1 were order remarks only; LCSC had 38 LSM6DSOTR on order day; the LSM6DSOX shares WHO_AM_I 0x6C so a substitution passes the driver check; `sourcing.md` expects about one board in five to need rework. Add to bring-up: ST's accelerometer and gyro self-test (CTRL5_C), the placement-image check of U1 pin 1, and a plan for a dead IMU on one of five boards.
9. **Use the Apple Watch files.** The two Apple Watch research-motion files from the eFoil sessions (100 Hz, user acceleration, gravity, rotation rate, Apple's attitude quaternion; 208 k and 140 k samples) are the only real 6-DOF IMU streams on hand. Fed through `wavemath.py` at fs 100 (f = (ua + g)·9.80665) they test the Mahony filter against Apple's quaternion on a real MEMS stream and check that the quality gate nulls the band-limited "Hs" (0.10–0.21 m with Tp pinned at the band edge) on quiet minutes. Zero hardware, this week.
10. **REQ-3 and the SOG window.** The first result comes at 551 s (6 segments); §5 item 5 (measure ODR for 120 s before building the processor) would move it to about 11 min on every boot, so do it only when the measured rate differs. The SOG window keeps 14 hop maxima (1103 s) while the 14-segment spectrum spans 15 hops (1181.5 s): the oldest hop has no SOG. Keep 15.
11. **Buoy feasibility.** Nothing on hand states the cruising ground; Queensland Waverider buoys sit offshore while anchorages are sheltered, so the review's reference may not share the anchorage's exposure. Keep a forecast-based fallback with its own threshold (OpenCPN's grib_pi reads WW3 wave fields inside GFS GRIBs) and store the series beside `/waves.csv`.
12. **OSP and the order of soldering.** `DESIGN.md` §12a says to fit the XIAOs and J4 leads soon after arrival; the review's order puts the J4 pigtail after calibration. Fit the J4 pigtail with the XIAO, on arrival. The XIAO-fitting notes in the no-storage order package say nothing sits under U4; on the ordered full build U5 and six passives sit under it on the bottom side, so mind iron heat and flux on the castellations above U5, and check U5 pin 1 on JLCPCB's placement images.
13. **`environment.heave` has no named consumer**, no sign convention in the spec, and is a sample-and-hold of the last FIFO sample every 250 ms rather than an average; its only acceptance test is "rocking moves it". Say what it is for, or publish it at the hop rate as RMS.
